# Executive Meeting Overview

Step E strengthens the AI Executive Meeting Simulation by moving beyond monthly report reading to strategic, multi-agent debate and CEO decision-making.

## Purpose

- Elevate AI executive meetings into true strategic discussion forums.
- Enable AI CFO, COO, CMO, and CHRO roles to debate key themes.
- Generate comparable decision options and capture final CEO judgments.
- Reflect decisions in next month projections and dashboard summaries.

## Architecture

- Monthly Simulation → Executive Meeting Engine
- Agents: CFO, COO, CMO, CHRO
- Strategic Debate Engine
- Decision Options Generator
- CEO Decision API → Next Month Simulation
- Executive Narrative / Minutes

## Key Outcomes

- Meeting agenda generation with financial, operational, portfolio, and issue perspectives.
- Agent opening statements, cross-discussion, and consensus summaries.
- A/B/C decision options with actions, pros, and cons.
- Decision reflection in future month projections.
- Meeting minutes stored for dashboard and narrative integration.
